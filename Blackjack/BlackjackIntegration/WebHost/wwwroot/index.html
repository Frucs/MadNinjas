HOW TO PLAY

Closest to 21 wins.

  1. Bet between 1 kr and your full balance. You and the dealer each receive two cards. One dealer card is hidden.
  2. Cards 2–10 are worth their face value. Jacks, queens and kings are worth 10. Aces count as 1 or 11, whichever is best for the hand.
  3. Blackjack is an ace and a card worth 10 as your first two cards. The round ends immediately. If only you have blackjack, you win 1.5 times your bet, rounded down. If both have blackjack, the round is a tie.
  4. Choose Hit or Stand. At 21, your turn ends. Going over 21 is an immediate loss.
  5. The dealer reveals the hidden card and draws until reaching at least 17. The dealer also stands on 17 with an ace counted as 11.
  6. A regular win returns your bet plus an equal amount in winnings. A tie returns your bet. A loss costs your bet.
  7. You start with 100 kr. The session ends when you choose to leave or your balance reaches zero. A new shuffled deck is created before a round if fewer than 15 cards remain.

Doubling, splitting and insurance are not included. Completed sessions are saved by the C# server in a shared JSON leaderboard.
